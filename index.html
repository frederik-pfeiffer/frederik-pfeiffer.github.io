Frederik Pfeiffer

Doctoral Student · Department of Political Science, University of Gothenburg

I am a PhD candidate in Political Science at the University of Gothenburg, starting in fall 2022. My research focuses on deliberative democracy, participation, and democratic attitudes, with a particular emphasis on climate and environmental politics.

My work is both empirical — with a strong focus on survey experiments and field studies — and theoretical, engaging with conceptualisations of legitimacy, political efficacy, and equality. Much of my recent research has centred on participatory and deliberative processes in Sweden, conducting participant surveys at deliberative mini-publics and citizen juries.

In fall 2025, I was affiliated with the Department of Political Science at Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz, Germany, as a guest researcher in the Chair of Political Theory and Public Policy, held by Prof. Claudia Landwehr.

I hold a BSc in International Economics and European Studies from the University of Tübingen and an MSc in Comparative Public Policy and Welfare Studies from the University of Southern Denmark. Beyond research, I give talks at schools and occasionally advise city officials on deliberative processes.

I teach Master-level courses on environmental politics and supervise Bachelor and Master theses. I have supervised 10 master theses and 6 Bachelor thesis since starting at University of Gothenburg. Students interested in supervision are welcome to reach out.
